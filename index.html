Our Core Vision

Empowering Pastors, Strengthening Churches

The Mindanao Pastors Group exists to foster spiritual unity, leadership excellence, and collaborative outreach across the island of Mindanao.

Pastoral Fellowship

Creating safe regional hubs where ministers can build genuine friendships, pray for one another, and prevent ministry burnout.

Youth & Leader Camps

Organizing annual youth retreats, leadership camps, and discipleship conferences to train the next generation of Christian leaders.

Mindanao Mission Outreach

Combining resources to support rural church plantings, disaster relief efforts, and community assistance programs in remote provinces.
